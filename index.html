Jihong Guan*, Jiaqi Wang*, Wengen Li, Hanchen Yang, Yichao Zhang, Shuigeng Zhou
Preprint, 2026
Paper Code

Research

Broadly, I am interested in structured prediction and generation: how models can reason over relations, generate coherent sets of outputs, and retain useful information over long-term interaction.
